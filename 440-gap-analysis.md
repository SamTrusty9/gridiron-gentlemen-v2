# 440andfriends.com — Gap Analysis vs Gridiron Gentlemen v2

Researched Oct 6, 2026 (read-only). 440 is a 10-manager Yahoo league site on Wix,
hand-illustrated flat-cartoon art system, almost entirely manually updated.

## What they have that v2 lacks (ranked by league value)

1. **Squid Game HQ** — branded season-long elimination survival game: live projections,
   survival-odds bars, chopping-block card, duel tokens, themed page. Marquee side game.
2. **Illustrated Trophy Room** — per-manager digital trophy shelves (trophies w/ year bases,
   hanging medals, framed jerseys, rivalry cups).
3. **Power Lines** — animated standings-position bump chart w/ "play the season" animation,
   draggable week slider, per-team trace.
4. **Draft grading** — per-pick grades, overall draft scores, best/worst drafts & picks,
   most top-5/bottom-5 picks, first-overall history.
5. **Championship box scores** — illustrated fantasy-box-score recaps of each title game,
   reached via clickable hanging championship banners.
6. **Named weekly bonus categories** (15, e.g. "The Sheriff", "Bench Warmer") + Weekly
   Winners cards + bonus-earnings "Bank" column in standings w/ breakdown popup.
7. **Dot Pot** — weekly position-high-scorer tracker (QB/RB/WR/TE) w/ per-manager table.
8. **Illustrated Hall of Records cards** — record cards w/ top-5 lists (blowout, highest
   combined, streaks, season high/low).
9. **Season Champions table** — every way to win a season (in-season, points, all-play).
10. **Rivalry Week** — annual rivalry matchups w/ custom trophies, origin stories, bets.
11. **Playful constitution** w/ quirky last-place penalty ("The Plate").
12. **League podcast** hosted on site.
13. **Manager profiles** — career stat cards w/ league rank on every stat.
14. **Updates forum** — rich weekly write-up posts + polls + comments.
15. **Consistent custom illustration art system** across every page.
16. **Medals Wall of Fame** — year-by-year position medal winners.

## They do poorly
- Stale "UPDATED 8/28/24" freshness marker; Wix iframes load blank for seconds;
  no real manager photos (avatars only); thin single-purpose pages.

## We already do better
- 12 franchise pages w/ photos, H2H matrix, lineup explorer; player search across
  seasons; bets tracker; playoff brackets; divisions; selfies wall; newsletter archive;
  deeper draft history (theirs pre-2024 is "coming soon").

## Build direction for v2 (our own design, never copying theirs)
Priority: trophy room w/ per-manager shelves, illustrated HOF record cards, draft
grading, championship banners → box-score recaps, weekly awards layer, power-lines
chart, rivalry week features. Art: warm sticker-book style already established.
